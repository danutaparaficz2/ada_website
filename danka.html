Professional Bio

Dr. Danuta (Danka) Paraficz is a distinguished AI Research Scientist at Fernfachhochschule Schweiz (FFHS), where she applies advanced machine learning and deep learning architectures to solve complex, real-world challenges. With a background rooted in high-level astrophysics and industrial data science, she bridges the gap between theoretical research and scalable, live production environments.

Danuta’s career is defined by a transition from exploring the mysteries of the universe to optimizing green energy through data: Energy Sector (DXT Commodities): For over seven years, she served as a Data Scientist in the energy trading sector. She developed forecasting models using satellite and meteorological data to predict wind and solar production across Europe. Her work in probabilistic forecasting and automated pricing systems helped position her team as a leader in energy forecasting.

Astrophysics Research (EPFL & LAM): As a Postdoctoral Researcher at EPFL and the Laboratoire d'Astrophysique de Marseille, Danuta specialized in gravitational lensing and dark matter. She developed scientific software and led international collaborations using high-performance computing clusters and advanced statistical methods.

Education & Academic Contributions PhD in Astronomy and Astrophysics: University of Copenhagen (Niels Bohr Institute). Her thesis on strong gravitational lensing introduced a new theoretical approach to measuring dark matter.
