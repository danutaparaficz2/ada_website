EAGLE

A Deep Learning Approach to Photovoltaics Reliability

EAGLE addresses the critical decarbonization challenge: ensuring that photovoltaic systems meet and exceed their 25-year lifespan. As high-efficiency modules (PERC, HJT, TOPCon) lack long-term track records, automated, scalable diagnostic tools are vital.

Methodology & Hardware: We utilize the PhaseOne iXM-MV150 (150 Mpx) multispectral sensor. This unique setup allows for the acquisition of 7-channel images, fusing Visible (RGB), Electroluminescence (EL), and Ultraviolet Fluorescence (UVf) data. This multi-bandwidth approach enables the tracking of degradation modes—such as moisture ingress and silver busbar oxidation—that are invisible to single-camera setups.

Neural Architecture Evolution

Initial research utilized Detectron2 for cell segmentation; however, current ADA research has transitioned to the Segment Anything Model (SAM) by Meta AI for zero-shot robustness across varied module architectures. For failure classification, we deploy ChannelViT (a specialized Vision Transformer), which processes each spectral channel independently to maximize accuracy in identifying six specific defect labels: good, crack, dark, corrosion, discolouration, and delamination.

Collaborations: In synergy with the ACHILLES project, EAGLE also investigates hail resistance, using high-resolution EL imaging to detect micro-defects at the cell level that trigger crack propagation during hailstorms.

PRONTO

Predicting Potato Sprouting to Optimise Tuber Storage

PRONTO is an Innosuisse-funded project (CHF 953k) addressing a critical gap in the food supply chain. Since the banning of the anti-sprouting agent CIPC in the EU/Switzerland (2020), alternative treatments have proven more expensive and less effective. Over 90% of potatoes require long-term storage, where sprouting leads to significant weight and quality loss.

The Innovation: Our solution exploits the electrophysiological signals of tubers. Using PhytlSigns Researcher sensors, we monitor internal plant signals interpreted through Supervised Machine Learning. This allows for a prediction window up to 3 weeks before visual symptoms appear.

Explainable AI (XAI) Framework

To ensure industrial adoption, we integrate XAI frameworks (LIME, SHAP) and uncertainty quantification. This provides farmers and storage managers (like fenaco and Zweifel) with a "reasoning path" behind the model's alerts, preventing unnecessary chemical applications and maximizing trust in the system.

Consortium: ADA Institute (FFHS) leads the algorithm development and treatment data analysis in collaboration with Vivent SA, Agroscope, SUPSI, and industry implementation partners.

RAG for N.O.T.

KI trifft Astronomie: Efficient Knowledge Access

Modern astronomy at the Nordic Optical Telescope (NOT) on La Palma generates immense amounts of technical documentation, scientific logs, and observation protocols. Accessing this information quickly is vital for telescope operations and research efficiency.

Implementation Strategy: ADA is developing a domain-specific Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system. Unlike standard LLMs, this system cites original observatory sources, ensuring that technical queries are answered with scientific transparency and zero hallucination.

Phase AFE1

Cloud-based RAG development for natural language querying of PDF archives and technical manuals.

Phase AFE2

Scaling to process numerical sensor data, tables, and integrated image recognition for astronomical charts.

Project Lead: Dr. Danuta Paraficz. Leveraging state-of-the-art tools including Gemini API and RAG-Anything.
