Professional Bio

Ralf Jandl is Research Field Lead for Energy, Environment, and Materials at the Laboratory for Web Science (LWS), Fernfachhochschule Schweiz (FFHS).

His professional background started in software development and evolved into software team leadership, project management, and consulting. He combines technical depth with a strong focus on practical delivery and stakeholder-oriented execution.

At FFHS, he contributes to applied initiatives that connect data-driven methods with sustainability-related domains, especially where digital innovation can support robust decision-making.

His academic background includes a Bachelor's degree in Business Information Technology, an MAS in Web4Business, and an MSc in Artificial Intelligence.
