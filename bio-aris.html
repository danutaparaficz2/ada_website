Professional Bio

Dr. Aris Marcolongo is a Senior AI Scientist at Fernfachhochschule Schweiz (FFHS), specializing in applied machine learning and practical AI systems.

His work focuses on designing robust models that connect scientific rigor with real-world deployment. He contributes to interdisciplinary projects where explainability, reliability, and measurable impact are central requirements.

At FFHS, he supports the development of end-to-end solutions, from data preparation and model design to evaluation and operational integration. His profile combines research depth with implementation-oriented thinking in collaborative project environments.
