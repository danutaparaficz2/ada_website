Professional Bio

Natasa Sarafijanovic-Djukic is a seasoned researcher and data scientist currently specializing in Applied Machine Learning at Fernfachhochschule Schweiz (FFHS). With a career spanning over a decade across academia and industry, she brings deep expertise in developing scalable data solutions and intelligent systems.

Since joining FFHS in January 2021, Natasa has focused her research on practical applications of machine learning. Her professional journey includes significant roles in both the public and private sectors: Industrial Machine Learning - previously served as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Research Fellow at Iris Technology Solutions, where she focused on data science for industrial applications, Data Science & Privacy - worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at Inria in France, in the field of web privacy and user tracking.

Expertise: Her technical repertoire includes Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing and RAG systems, time series data modelling, interpretable machine learning.

Education & Academic Excellence: Natasa holds a PhD in Computer and Communication Science from EPFL (École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne), one of the world’s leading technical institutions. She is recognized by colleagues for being highly detail-oriented and a quick learner, with a strong foundation in probability and statistics.
